# What is 0xMarkets

0xMarkets is a **non-custodial, multi-asset perpetual futures exchange** on Base. Trade FX, commodities, and crypto and more with leverage, using **USDC as collateral**, straight from your own wallet — permissionless, trustless.

The exchange is live in **Beta**: fully functional, and actively being refined as it matures.

## What's a perpetual future?

A **perpetual future** ("perp") is a contract that tracks the price of an asset — say gold or BTC — without an expiry date. You take a position on whether the price goes **up (long)** or **down (short)**, and your profit or loss follows the price as it moves. You never own the underlying asset; you're trading its price, with leverage, so a small amount of collateral can control a much larger position.

## Who you trade against

There's no order book matching you to another trader. Instead, you trade against a pool of liquidity supplied by **liquidity providers (LPs)** — they are the other side of every position. Prices come from a real-time oracle (**Pyth Pro** at launch), so you always trade at the market price adjusted for the size of your trade.

The same person can be both a trader and an LP — providing liquidity and taking positions are separate roles, and you're free to do either or both.

## What you can trade

Three asset classes, all collateralized in USDC:

- **FX** — major currency pairs
- **Commodities** — gold, silver, oil
- **Crypto** — BTC, ETH, and more

See the full list in [Markets](../reference/markets.md).

## Why it's different

- **Self-custody.** Your funds stay in your wallet. You sign every action; 0xMarkets never takes custody.
- **No KYC, no account.** Connect a wallet and trade.
- **One collateral, many markets.** USDC backs every position across FX, commodities, and crypto.
