# Connect your wallet

0xMarkets is **non-custodial** — you keep your funds in your own wallet at all times. There's no account to create and no deposit to a 0xMarkets-controlled address. You connect a wallet, sign your trades, and your collateral stays under your control.

## What you need

- An **EVM wallet** (a standard Ethereum-compatible wallet). 0xMarkets works with common EVM wallets — Coinbase Wallet and other widely-used wallets — through a standard wallet connector.
- That wallet set to the **Base network**.
- A small amount of **ETH on Base** to pay for gas (network transaction fees).

## Steps

1. Open the 0xMarkets app and choose to connect a wallet. 
2. Pick your wallet from the list and approve the connection request in your wallet. This is a read-only connection — it does not move any funds.
3. Make sure your wallet is on the **Base network**. If it isn't, switch to Base — most wallets will prompt you to switch or add the network automatically when you connect. 

Once connected, your address shows in the app and you're ready to fund it.

## A note on gas

Every on-chain action you take — opening a position, closing one, cancelling an order — needs a small amount of **ETH on Base** to cover network gas. Keep a little ETH in your wallet so your transactions don't fail for lack of gas. The keepers that execute your trades are paid by the protocol, so there's no separate keeper fee for you to cover; see [Deposit USDC](deposit-usdc.md).
