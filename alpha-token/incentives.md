# Incentives / Campaigns

**10% of ALPHA emissions** flow into a flexible **Incentive Pool**, used to bootstrap activity, reward participation, and run targeted ecosystem programs. The pool is intentionally not earmarked: governance and the team can deploy it wherever it has the highest leverage.

## What the Incentive Pool Funds

| Program | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Weekly Trader Leaderboards** | Reward active traders by volume / PnL — drives consistent trading activity |
| **Introducing Broker (IB) Rebates** | Compensate affiliates and partners bringing volume to the protocol |
| **Airdrops** | Targeted distributions to early users, community members, or strategic counterparties |
| **Community Programs** | Engagement initiatives, hackathons, ecosystem grants, and growth experiments |

Allocation between programs is flexible and re-evaluated as the protocol grows.

## Weekly Trader Leaderboards

* Trade volume and activity generate **points**
* Points convert to ALPHA from the incentive pool at the end of each epoch
* Rewards are swept to participant wallets via the **liquidity flow controller**
* Leaderboard live at [liquidity.0xmarkets.io/leaderboard](https://liquidity.0xmarkets.io/leaderboard)

Leaderboard windows follow the same **Friday → Thursday UTC** cycle as Cartha epochs.

## Introducing Broker (IB) Program

The IB program rewards affiliates for **referred trading volume**:

* Create a referral code on the Referrals page
* Share your code/link with traders
* Earn a percentage of fees from their trades, paid in USDC
* Higher tiers unlock with weekly active-user and volume thresholds

Full referral mechanics, tiers, and discount/rebate table: [Referrals](../referrals.md).

IB rebates are **funded from the same 10% incentive pool**, but paid out in USDC for the affiliate's convenience.

## Airdrops & Community Programs

The pool's flexibility lets the protocol respond to opportunities — for example:

* Airdrops to specific cohorts (early traders, early LPs, partner-protocol users)
* Hackathon prize pools
* Targeted grants to integrations, tooling builders, or content creators
* Bonus emissions for under-served pools to attract liquidity

Specific campaigns are announced through the [official channels](../contact.md).

## How the Pool Stays Funded

The 10% emission allocation is automatic — every epoch, the pool grows by ~720 ALPHA/day worth of issuance. Unused balance carries forward, so a quiet epoch leaves more powder for the next campaign.

## What This Doesn't Cover

The incentive pool is **separate** from:

* **LP emissions** (31% of ALPHA — to miners by deposit score)
* **Validator emissions** (41% of ALPHA — for liquidation execution)
* **Trading-fee distribution** (a different stream entirely — see [Fee Distribution](rewards/fee-distribution.md))

> Specific campaigns, eligibility, and reward formulas are announced when they launch. Past campaigns don't guarantee future ones.
