# LP Vaults

Liquidity on 0xMarkets is provided through **USDC vaults on Base Mainnet**. Each market has its own vault, and vaults are grouped by asset class under a **parent vault** that handles routing and shared accounting.

## Why One Vault Per Market

Vault isolation means **risk in one market does not bleed into another**. A bad-debt event on, say, the WTI market draws from that market's vault and the insurance pool — not from BTC or EUR LPs.

This isolation is the core design tradeoff: LPs choose where to deploy capital, and their exposure is bounded by that choice.

## How Deposits Work

1. **Choose a market vault** that you want to back (e.g. cvBTC, cvEUR)
2. **Lock USDC** for a duration of your choosing — longer locks earn time bonuses
3. **Earn rewards** from epoch start ([Weekly Epochs](../how-it-works/weekly-epochs.md))

Your reward score for the epoch is:

```
score = locked_amount × time_factor × pool_weight
```

* **`locked_amount`** — USDC committed at the freeze (mid-epoch top-ups go to the next epoch)
* **`time_factor`** — increases with lock duration
* **`pool_weight`** — set by veALPHA Alpha Voting (see [Alpha Token › Rewards](../alpha-token/rewards/README.md))

## What LPs Earn

* **50% of trading fees** generated by the market you backed (paid in USDC)
* **31% of ALPHA emissions** from the Cartha subnet, allocated by deposit score

See [Fee Distribution](../alpha-token/rewards/fee-distribution.md) for the full breakdown.

## What LPs Are Exposed To

* **Trader PnL** — if traders win, the vault pays out; if traders lose, the vault grows
* **Liquidation shortfalls** — covered first by the insurance pool, then the vault if the pool is exhausted
* **Lock duration** — funds are committed until the lock expires *and* the 7-day cooldown ends

See [Risk Management](risk-management.md) for the full risk surface and mitigations.
