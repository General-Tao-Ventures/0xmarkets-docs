# 0xMarkets Liquidity Provider

**The 0xMarkets Liquidity Provider** is the decentralized liquidity backbone for 0xMarkets, built on the **Cartha Subnet (SN35)** on Bittensor. 0xMarkets is a **multi-asset decentralized perpetual futures exchange** that offers traders up to **500x leverage** on currencies, commodities, crypto, and other RWAs in a permissionless manner.

**Repositories:**

* **Miner CLI**: [cartha-cli](https://github.com/General-Tao-Ventures/cartha-cli)
* **Validator**: [cartha-validator](https://github.com/General-Tao-Ventures/cartha-validator)

## What is the 0xMarkets Liquidity Provider?

The 0xMarkets Liquidity Provider runs on Cartha, a Bittensor subnet where miners act as **Liquidity Providers (LPs)** for the 0xMarkets DEX. It provides:

* **USDC-only collateral** with one vault per market for risk isolation
* **ALPHA emissions** distributed across participants (31% to miners, 41% to validators, 10% to incentive pool, 18% owner)
* **50% of trading fees** distributed to miners (LPs)
* **40% of trading fees** to veALPHA stakers
* **10% of trading fees** to protocol treasury
* **Liquidation monitoring** by validators with slashing penalties

## What Can You Do with the 0xMarkets Liquidity Provider?

### For Miners (Liquidity Providers)

* **Deposit USDC** into designated liquidity vaults to earn rewards
* **Choose specific vaults/pairs** to support (BTC/USD, ETH/USD, TAO/USD, GOLD/USD, SILVER/USD, EUR/USD, GBP/USD, JPY/USD)
* **Earn 31% of ALPHA emissions** based on your deposit score (time × amount × pool weight)
* **Earn 50% of trading fees** generated by the 0xMarkets protocol
* **Use the 0xMarkets Liquidity Interface** for a streamlined lock/unlock experience
* **View and manage positions** through the web interface
* **Extend locks and top up** existing positions
* **Commit funds by epoch durations** for optimized returns

### For Validators

* **Monitor markets** for liquidation events
* **Execute liquidations** when margin requirements are breached
* **Earn 41% of ALPHA emissions** for subnet maintenance
* **Earn 20% of liquidation fees** for successful executions
* **Post ALPHA collateral** to participate in liquidation execution
* **Maintain validator infrastructure** with slashing penalties for missed liquidations

### For Traders

* **Trade on 0xMarkets** with up to 500x leverage on FX, commodities, and crypto
* **Earn from incentive pool** through weekly leaderboard rewards
* **Introducing Broker program** earn rebates for referred trading volume
* **Track rewards** on the leaderboard at https://liquidity.0xmarkets.io/leaderboard
* **Permissionless trading** with fixed fee structure

### For Developers

* **Integrate with smart contracts** on Base Mainnet
* **Use the 0xMarkets Liquidity Interface** for testing frontend integration
* **Develop integration tools** and automation scripts
* **Use API endpoints** and verifier functionality
* **Build monitoring dashboards** and analytics tools

## Network Configuration

### Network Details

* **Bittensor Network**: `finney` (Bittensor mainnet)
* **Subnet UID**: `35` (Cartha subnet)
* **EVM Chain**: Base Mainnet (Chain ID: `8453`)
* **Lock UI URL**: `https://liquidity.0xmarkets.io` (for managing locks and positions)

### Token Requirements

* **TAO**: Required for subnet registration
* **ETH**: Required for gas fees on Base Mainnet
* **USDC**: Required for liquidity provision
